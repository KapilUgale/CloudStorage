About Us

StoreIt is a next-gen file storage platform that combines the power of cloud technology with the security and transparency of blockchain. Our mission is to provide users with a safe, decentralized, and globally accessible file storage solution.

With StoreIt, your files are encrypted, stored across multiple nodes, and are accessible from anywhere in the world. Every action is recorded on the blockchain, ensuring trust and immutability.

Blockchain Security

Every file is hashed and logged securely on the blockchain to prevent tampering or loss.

Access Anywhere

Access your files from any device, anytime – with just a login.

Decentralized Storage

Your data is stored across a distributed network to avoid single point failures.

User-Friendly Interface

Simple and clean UI designed to make file storage easy and efficient for everyone.
